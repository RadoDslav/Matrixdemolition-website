# Pool Demolition and Removal Contractors in Dallas/Fort Worth

Matrix Demolition provides professional pool demolition and removal throughout the DFW Metroplex for old pools, new construction, renovation, and landscaping projects.

## Services include

- In-ground pool demolition for concrete, vinyl, and fiberglass pools.
- Deck and patio demolition.
- Pool equipment removal including pumps, filters, and heaters.
- Site preparation for landscaping.

## Process

1. Initial consultation and site assessment.
2. Permitting, utility coordination, and site preparation.
3. Safe demolition and removal.
4. Debris cleanup, recycling, and site restoration.

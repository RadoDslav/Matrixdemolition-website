# Frac Sand Mining Contractors Texas

Matrix Demolition clears land and uses heavy machinery to extract silica sand. The source positions the company as a Texas-wide provider focused on safe, reliable work, schedule, budget, and environmental impact.

## Services include

### Land clearing

Remove vegetation and topsoil to access the silica sand deposit.

### Excavation

Excavate deep into the earth to extract silica sand.

### Haul-off roads

Level ground, create haul-off roads, and construct berms and drainage ditches.

### Screening

Use vibrating screens and classifiers to separate silica sand by particle size.

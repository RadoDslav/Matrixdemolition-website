# Structural Demolition Services

Matrix Demolition handles large multi-story commercial structures, single-story homes, temporary structures, and inner-city buildings. The family-owned team has more than 30 years of experience and uses structural demolition techniques with current equipment.

## Services include

Residential demolition · Commercial demolition · Complete demolition · Selective and gut-out demolition · Interior demolition · Exterior demolition · Pre-construction services · Site preparation.

The source says the company is certified to remove asbestos and prioritizes safe, efficient work that follows relevant guidelines and health and safety procedures.

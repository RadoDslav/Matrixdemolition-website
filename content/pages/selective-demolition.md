# Selective Demolition Services

Selective demolition maintains an existing structure’s integrity while removing dated or non-functional components. Strategic sequencing separates interior components, minimizes waste, decreases project length, and lowers overall cost.

## Services include

Residential demolition · Commercial demolition · Complete demolition · Selective and gut-out demolition · Interior demolition · Exterior demolition · Pre-construction services · Site preparation.

Matrix Demolition has completed selective demolition projects in DFW and surrounding areas since opening in 1989. Recyclable materials may be reused to reduce environmental impact.
